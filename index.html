Our Story

About New Sania Jewellers

Nestled in Mashallah Market, Aliabad, in the heart of Hazara Town, Quetta, New Sania Jewellers is a neighbourhood jewelry house where every guest is welcomed like family. We believe a piece of jewelry is never just an ornament — it is a memory waiting to be worn.

From delicate everyday gold to statement pieces for weddings and celebrations, our shelves are arranged for you to browse slowly, ask questions freely, and choose with complete confidence. Visit us in person, or simply send a message — we are happy to share photographs, details and prices before you ever leave home.
